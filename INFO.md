# 7 Wonders Duel

A two-player game of drafting cards from a shared pyramid to build a city and raise wonders. Play
through three Ages, trade with the bank, and win by military supremacy, scientific supremacy, or on
points after the third Age. If a player leaves mid-game, the opponent wins by forfeit.

The Agora, Pantheon and Extra Wonders expansions are not part of this edition.
